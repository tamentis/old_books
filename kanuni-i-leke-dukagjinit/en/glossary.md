Terms left in Albanian because I could not identify them:
- *babune*: a measure of wheat (§45), and of the miller's toll (§387).
- *strufulleca*: a role at the wedding (§46 and §49).
- *koshiq*: a measure of grain, perhaps a small basket (from *kosh*) (§319).
- *perzivoj* and *kariqe*: kinds of fish trap (§437).
- *rrjell*: apparently something used to stun fish (§444–446).
- *huta*: the Martini rifle (§484).
- *kashata*: perhaps a round of cheese or of bread (§601 j, §663–664). In §663 *nyjen* is the verb "cuts", not a second item.
- *bracat* and *biramelat*: apparently minors and the simple-minded (§768, §774–776).
- *hànor* and *vergan*: kinds of favoured animal that may not be raided (§799).
- *Razogj*: the "two meals of bread" owed to the House of Gjomarku (§1142).
- *lima*: something belonging to the House of Gjomarku in house-burnings (§1145).
